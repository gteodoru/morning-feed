🍂 Woodstock, Vermont

Thu Oct 1 → Mon Oct 5, 2026 · 2 adults, 1 au pair & a 22-month-old
Peak foliage window · Billings Harvest Celebration weekend

🧳 Packing & prep

  • Warm layers for the toddler: fleece, hat, mittens, puffy jacket (nights drop to ~35–45°F)
  • Waterproof shoes + rain suit for farm mud
  • Carrier + umbrella stroller (farms & carriage roads are stroller-friendly, trails aren't)
  • White noise machine, travel crib, blackout shades if you have them
  • Snacks for the car: ~4h drive each way with at least one stop
  • Book lodging NOW — foliage season sells out early and prices spike
  • Buy Billings Farm Harvest Celebration tickets in advance (faster entry)
  • Dinner reservations: Woodstock Inn area spots book out on foliage weekends

🌦️ What to expect

Foliage: central VT peaks roughly Oct 7–12 — you're catching the very start of peak, which means vivid early color without the absolute crush of Columbus weekend crowds.
Weather: expect 55–65°F days, 35–45°F nights. Layers and a warm hat for the toddler are non-negotiable.
Toddler rhythm: every day is built around one nap (~12:30–2:30) — one morning outing, lunch, nap, one lazy afternoon outing. With the au pair along, tag-team any evening you want adults-only.
Rain backup: Montshire Museum of Science in Norwich (~20 min) has a dedicated preschool area; VINS in Quechee is mostly under cover.
Built for the family · drive: Old Greenwich → Woodstock ≈ 4h via I-95 → I-91 N
